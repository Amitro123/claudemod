# Tool calls per agent, measured (2026-11-19)

Where the factory's default `budgets` (`hooks/config.ts`) come from. `/retro:agent-audit` measured every
factory agent of one session from its own transcript (`<session>/subagents/agent-<id>.jsonl`), counting work
calls and not the hand-back. The session held a review of #149, two full runs of the board's confirm feature
(the first lost to a restart after its reviews) and a full run of issue #152.

| Role | Runs | Calls seen | Default budget | Stop at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| intake | 3 | 5-6 | 12 | 24 |
| spec-writer | 3 | 12-14 | 30 | 60 |
| builder | 4 | 12-47 | 120 | 240 |
| security reviewer | 4 | 5-8 | 20 | 40 |
| code reviewer | 4 | 7-12 | 25 | 50 |
| approver | 3 | 1-5 | 12 | 24 |
| skeptic | 1 | 32 | 60 | 120 |
| extra reviewer (ux, ui-design) | 2 | 3-6 | 25 | 50 |

**The rule.** A budget is about twice the most calls seen, so a normal run never meets it and the stop at twice
the budget catches a loop at about four times a normal run. The builder's is wider: these were medium features,
and a large one needs more. The skeptic's rests on one run.

**What these runs don't show.**
- Nothing about a large feature's builder.
- Nothing about a sonnet intake or approver: every run here used opus for both. A model change waits for runs
  on both models (#156).

**How to measure again.** Run `/retro:agent-audit --all` in a project where the factory ran, and look at "Budgets
from what was observed" for the `factory:*` types.
